NZOChain integrates with widely used Web3 wallets so you can activate threat protection without changing how you transact. Your existing wallet stays in place — NZOChain connects alongside it to scan your activity and alert you when risk signals appear.

## Supported wallets

| Wallet | Type | Chains (scan product) | Connection |
|--------|------|--------|----------|
| MetaMask | Browser extension | EVM (4 networks) | Direct |
| WalletConnect | Protocol | EVM via WC | QR / deep link |
| Trust Wallet | Mobile | EVM via WC | WalletConnect |
| Coinbase Wallet | Browser / mobile | EVM via WC | WalletConnect |

**Solana is not supported.** Phantom / Solana do **not** enter the current scan product for the 4 EVM chains. If the UI lists Phantom, treat it as a connector only — not Solana coverage in the engine.

MetaMask uses a direct browser extension connection. WalletConnect-based wallets (Trust Wallet, Coinbase Wallet) connect via QR code or deep link. After connection, NZOChain can run **on-demand** scans. **Scheduled 6-hour re-scan is opt-in and off by default** — connecting a wallet does not by itself enable continuous or automatic monitoring, and NZOChain does not intercept the mempool at signing time.

## How to connect your wallet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the NZOChain dashboard">
    Go to your NZOChain dashboard and click **Connect Wallet** in the top navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your wallet">
    Choose your wallet from the list of supported providers. If you use a mobile wallet, select WalletConnect to generate a QR code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the read-only connection">
    Your wallet will prompt you to approve the connection. NZOChain only requests read access — no signing permission is requested or required for connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan starts">
    Once connected, NZOChain scans your wallet's activity, approvals, and contract interactions on the four supported EVM networks. Your RiskScore™ loads after the scan completes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Privacy and read-only access

NZOChain's connection to your wallet is strictly read-only. When you connect, NZOChain reads your on-chain history, active approvals, and contract interactions — it never requests the ability to sign transactions, move funds, or access your private key or seed phrase.

Your private key and seed phrase never leave your wallet. NZOChain does not store them, request them, or have any mechanism to access them. If any service claiming to be NZOChain asks for your seed phrase, do not provide it.

<Warning>
  NZOChain will never ask you to enter your seed phrase or sign a transaction during the connection process. Any prompt requesting this is a phishing attempt — disconnect immediately and report it.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Use on-demand scan or opt-in 6h monitoring to refresh risk signals. NZOChain does not provide continuous real-time / mempool blocking at the signing moment.
</Note>
