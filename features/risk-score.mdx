Your RiskScore™ is a composite number between 0 and 100 that reflects the current threat exposure of your wallet based on the latest scan. The score updates when you run an on-demand scan or when opt-in monitoring completes a scheduled re-scan (every 6 hours). A lower score means your wallet looks clean; a higher score means action is needed.

Scores are **advisory triage** — not a certified or definitive threat verdict.

## Score bands and what they mean

NZOChain maps every score to one of four bands, each with a clear recommended response.

| Score | Band | Status | Recommended action |
|-------|------|--------|--------------------|
| 0–30 | Safe | No significant threats detected | No action required — review periodically |
| 31–60 | Warning | Suspicious signals present | Review flagged approvals and contract interactions |
| 61–85 | High Risk | Dangerous approvals or contracts detected | Revoke risky permissions immediately |
| 86–100 | Critical | Active threats requiring immediate action | Act now — revoke all dangerous approvals and disconnect from suspicious dApps |

<Warning>
  A Critical score (86–100) means NZOChain has detected elevated threats against your wallet. Do not sign any new transactions until you have reviewed and resolved the flagged issues. Go to your **Approval Manager** and revoke dangerous permissions immediately.
</Warning>

## What contributes to your score

Your RiskScore™ is calculated from multiple threat signals. NZOChain weighs each factor based on severity and combines them into a single score via a **rules / heuristics engine**.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Active approvals" icon="key">
    Unlimited or high-value token approvals granted to external contracts. The more uncapped approvals you have, the higher your score.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Contract risk flags" icon="file-contract">
    Interactions with contracts identified as malicious, unverified, or associated with known exploit patterns.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Phishing signals" icon="fish">
    Connections to domains or contracts that match known phishing infrastructure, fake dApps, or address-spoofing patterns.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Behavioral anomalies" icon="chart-line">
    Unusual activity patterns such as rapid approval sequences or transactions to newly deployed contracts.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How scoring works (threat intel)

NZOChain uses a **rules engine + multi-source signals**:

- Proprietary triage rules (approvals, bytecode heuristics, connection patterns)
- **GoPlus** security flags
- Public denylists and known-bad address lists

This is **not** AI-powered analysis and **not** a fully proprietary ML model. Premium plans may unlock richer history and higher scan limits — not a separate “AI RiskScore” engine.

## RiskScore history

<Info>
  RiskScore history is available on the **Pro** and **Premium** plans.
</Info>

Pro and Premium users can view a timeline of their RiskScore over time. History lets you:

- Identify when your score changed and what caused the change
- Track the impact of revoking approvals or removing risky connections
- Review wallet activity for a period (personal audit — not legal forensics)

The history view is accessible from your dashboard under **Wallet Overview → Score History**.

## How scores update

Your RiskScore™ recalculates when:

- You run an on-demand scan
- Opt-in monitoring completes a **6-hour** scheduled re-scan and finds new signals
- Threat / denylist data used in the next scan includes new matches for your wallet's connections

It does **not** update in real time from mempool or push events. Refresh via scan or wait for the next monitor tick.
