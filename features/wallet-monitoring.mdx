NZOChain's wallet monitoring is **opt-in**. Once you enable it for a wallet, the platform runs a **scheduled re-scan every 6 hours** on the **four supported EVM chains**. It is **not** real-time, mempool, or event-driven continuous monitoring.

Alerts go out on the channels included in your [B2C plan](/pricing/individual).

## Alert channels

Different plans unlock additional alert channels. Choose the ones that fit how you work.

| Channel | Available on | Notes |
|---------|-------------|-------|
| Email | All B2C plans | Included on Free |
| Push | Basic+ | Browser / app |
| Telegram | Pro+ | Ligar em Definições → Notificações |

<Tip>
  Connect your Telegram account under **Settings → Notifications → Telegram**. Delivery timing depends on when the next scheduled scan completes and your mail/Telegram provider — not on mempool latency.
</Tip>

## How to add and manage monitored wallets

The number of wallets you can monitor depends on your plan:

- **Free**: 1 wallet
- **Basic**: 3 wallets
- **Pro**: 10 wallets
- **Premium**: 50 wallets

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your dashboard">
    Log in to NZOChain and navigate to **Wallets** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a wallet">
    Click **Add Wallet**, then paste the wallet address you want to monitor. You can add any public address — you do not need to connect it with a wallet extension to monitor it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select chains to monitor">
    By default NZOChain covers Ethereum, BNB Chain, Polygon and Arbitrum. Solana is **not supported**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable scheduled monitoring">
    Turn on opt-in auto-scan for the wallet. NZOChain will re-scan on the 6-hour schedule and notify you when new risk signals appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure alert preferences">
    Choose which alert types trigger notifications for this wallet under **Wallet Settings → Alerts**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Alert types

NZOChain generates targeted alerts so you know what a scheduled scan found and what to do next.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="New approval detected" icon="eye">
    Fires when a scan finds a new token approval from your wallet. Includes the contract address, token, and approval amount.
  </Card>
  <Card title="High-risk contract interaction" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Fires when a scan finds interaction with a contract flagged as malicious, unverified, or linked to known exploits.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Phishing / denylist match" icon="shield">
    Fires when a scan matches known phishing domains, fake dApps, or denylisted addresses.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Unusual activity pattern" icon="chart-line">
    Fires when heuristic anomalies are detected — such as rapid sequential approvals or interactions with newly deployed contracts.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Cross-chain monitoring

When you add a wallet, NZOChain can scan it on Ethereum, BNB Chain, Polygon and Arbitrum. You do not add the same address separately per chain.

Each alert identifies which chain the finding relates to.

## Monitoring frequency

| Mode | Behavior |
|------|----------|
| **Opt-in scheduled** | Re-scan every **6 hours** via cron |
| **On-demand** | Manual scan from the dashboard or API |

NZOChain does **not** watch the mempool and does **not** offer continuous event-driven monitoring. Expect alerts after the next successful scheduled (or manual) scan — not at transaction confirmation time.
