Transaction simulation lets you see **what a proposed transaction would do** to your wallet **before** you confirm it in MetaMask, WalletConnect, or another connector. It complements [RiskScore](/features/risk-score) and [contract analysis](/features/contract-analysis) when you are about to sign unfamiliar calldata.

<Info>
  NZOChain never signs on your behalf. Simulation is **read-only / advisory**. When live simulation is unavailable, you may receive **static or degraded analysis** instead of a full live execution preview.
</Info>

## How it works

1. You provide the transaction context (target contract, calldata, value, network) from the app or via the B2B API.
2. The platform runs a **live simulation** when the service is available.
3. If live simulation cannot run, NZOChain falls back to **static / degraded analysis** (still advisory).

Supported networks: **Ethereum, BNB Chain, Polygon, Arbitrum** (four EVM chains).

## B2C quotas (billing period)

Simulation usage on the consumer app is **metered per billing period**. One unit = **one simulation** consumed in that period.

| Plan | Simulations / period |
|------|----------------------|
| Free | 5 |
| Basic | 50 |
| Pro | 200 |
| Premium | 1 000 |

When you are logged in, the app shows how much of your plan limit you have used (`simulationsUsed` vs `simulationLimit` from your subscription). See [Individual plans](/pricing/individual) and [GET /api/plans](/api/plans).

Exhausting your B2C simulation quota blocks further simulations until the period resets or you upgrade.

## B2B API

`POST /api/v1/transactions/simulate` requires **Starter** or higher and a monthly simulation quota on the API plan. Details: [API v1](/api/v1) and [Enterprise pricing](/pricing/enterprise).

## When to use simulation

- Before approving a new spender on a site you do not fully trust.
- When calldata is opaque and your wallet only shows “Contract interaction”.
- After an incident — pair with [forensic analysis](/features/forensic-analysis) to avoid repeating the same mistake.

## Limits and errors

- B2B: `403 SIMULATION_QUOTA` when the key’s monthly simulate quota is used up.
- Forensic and scan endpoints have separate per-path rate limits; see [API overview](/api/overview).
